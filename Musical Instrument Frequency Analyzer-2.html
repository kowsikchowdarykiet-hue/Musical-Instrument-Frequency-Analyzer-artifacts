What does this mean?


Eigenvalues tell us the natural frequencies of the system.
Eigenvectors tell us the vibration patterns or modes.
Changing mass or stiffness changes the natural frequencies.




Mass
How heavy the system is.


Stiffness
How strongly the springs resist movement.


Natural frequency
The frequency at which the system naturally vibrates.


Vibration mode
A particular pattern of movement.
